THE CHALLENGE

High-Impact Passenger Visibility on a Curved Architectural Facade

Chennai Central Railway Station is one of India's busiest railway termini, accommodating hundreds of thousands of daily commuters. The station authority required a grand digital display above the concourse entrance that could broadcast dynamic train schedules, safety announcements, and high-revenue commercial brand campaigns simultaneously.

The mounting surface featured a gentle architectural curvature, requiring custom-angled modular cabinets that could maintain precise sub-millimeter pixel spacing without visible vertical gaps or image distortion along the curve.

ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Custom Angled Aluminium Cabinets & Dual-Processor Redundancy

  • CNC Curved Alignment: Engineered 12 interlocking die-cast aluminium cabinets with precision bevel cuts matching the exact radius of the entrance wall.
  • Daylight & Twilight Auto-Dimming: Integrated ambient optical sensors automatically adjust luminance from 6,500 nits during noon to 1,200 nits at night, preventing passenger glare while conserving energy.
  • Dual Power & Gigabit Signal Redundancy: Ensures that even in the event of an individual power circuit disruption, the display continues operating without dark zones or downtime.
PROJECT IMPACT

Uninterrupted Operation Since April 2024

Since its commissioning in April 2024, the Chennai Central display wall has maintained 100% operational uptime through heavy monsoon seasons and direct coastal sun exposure. It remains a flagship demonstration of ODLED's custom structural manufacturing and long-term reliability.
