THE SCREEN.

An LED display is not a single monitor; it is a synchronized matrix of optics, high-speed driver ICs, structural aluminium, and precision power regulation. Discover how ODLED engineers each layer for long-term reliability.

LAYER 01 / CORE COMPONENT

High-Density LED Module

The fundamental building block of ODLED video walls. Engineered with premium SMD LEDs, high-refresh driver ICs, and multi-layer copper PCBs to deliver true-to-life contrast, deep blacks, and exceptional color fidelity.

  • Available in pixel pitches from P1.25mm up to P10mm
  • 3840Hz+ ultra-high refresh rate for flicker-free camera capture
  • Gold-wire bonded SMD LED packages for 100,000-hour operational life
  • Front-serviceable magnetic module mounting for 10-second maintenance
CORE ENGINEERING PILLARS

PRECISION STANDARDS
& THERMAL DYNAMICS.

01

PWM Driver ICs & Refresh

Utilizes high-grade PWM constant-current driver ICs operating at 3,840Hz to 7,680Hz. Eliminates scanlines and moiré artifacts in professional broadcast studios and smartphone recordings.

02

Passive Convection Airflow

Die-cast aluminium cabinets act as high-efficiency heatsinks. Thermal channels naturally expel heat upward, eliminating noisy cooling fans and preventing dust ingress.

03

Photometric Calibration

Pixel-by-pixel brightness and chroma calibration ensures zero patchwork effect. Color deviation across panels is kept strictly below ΔE < 2 for seamless canvas uniformity.

04

92%+ SMPS Power Efficiency

Switched-Mode Power Supplies regulate electrical load with over-voltage, short-circuit, and thermal protections, significantly reducing facility power bills and operating temperatures.

05

IP65 Hermetic Weather Seal

Outdoor modules use conformal coated multi-layer PCBs sealed with UV-resistant silicone potting compound, fully protected against heavy rain, dust, and coastal salt mist.

06

Zero Mechanical Seam Tolerance

High-precision CNC alignment pins and quick-locking cam latches allow modular cabinets to join seamlessly with less than 0.05mm gap tolerance for a truly monolithic surface.
